Batman, also known as Bruce Wayne, is Gotham's protector. He has no superpowers and relies on training and technology. After witnessing his parents' murder, he dedicated his life to fighting crime. He is one of the most iconic superheroes ever created, and has led teams like the Justice League and the Bat Family. His greatest weapon is his mind, and his greatest burden is the vow he can never put down.

Richard John "Dick" Grayson was once Batman's first sidekick Robin, before going on to become Nightwing. Once he took up the mantle of Batman when Bruce was thought to be dead, but became Nightwing once again after the events of Flashpoint. He first appeared in Detective Comics #38 in April 1940. He was the youngest in a family of acrobats known as the Flying Graysons. After his parents were killed by a mafia boss named Tony Zucco, Bruce Wayne took him in and trained him as Robin. As he grew older, he became Nightwing and led teams like the Teen Titans and Outsiders.

Alfred Beagle was born as the son of Jarvis Beagle, a retired military agent who had proved to be less than apt as his father though a competent military officer. Following the service of the First World War, Beagle followed the deathbed request of his father who used to be a butler for the Wayne family. Alfred sought out Wayne to fulfill his father's last request and upon arriving to Gotham City, Alfred introduced himself to Bruce Wayne and Dick Grayson at Wayne Manor and insisted on becoming their butler.

The Huntress

Helena Wayne

Helena Wayne is the daughter of Batman and Catwoman from Earth-2. She became the Huntress after her mother died and worked to restore her honor. She later joined forces with Dick Grayson and became part of the Justice Society. She died during the Crisis on Infinite Earths, a sacrifice that echoed the legacy of both her parents. Fierce, principled, and deeply loyal, she carried two extraordinary legacies with a grace entirely her own.

Batwoman

Kathy Kane

A wealthy Gotham heiress and former circus performer, Kathy Kane became Batwoman inspired by Batman. She used gadgets disguised as makeup and jewelry, turning glamour into weaponry with inventive flair. She had romantic feelings for Batman but eventually retired from crime-fighting. Later, she was killed by the League of Assassins — a reminder that the life she chose was never without its costs.

The Original Bat-Girl

Bette Kane

Betty Kane is the niece of Kathy Kane, the first Batwoman. She was introduced as Bat-Girl as a romantic interest for Robin, appearing between 1961 and 1964... Betty Kane is the niece of Kathy Kane, the first Batwoman. She was introduced as Bat-Girl as a romantic interest for Robin. She appeared between 1961 and 1964 but was later removed as editors felt such characters were too distracting. She was later reintroduced and became associated with the Teen Titans, growing beyond her original role into a heroine of her own making — energetic, determined, and impossible to dismiss.

The Cat

Catwoman

Catwoman, also known as Selina Kyle, is a cat burglar from Gotham City. She is sometimes a villain and sometimes an ally to Batman, navigating both roles with a fluidity that makes her impossible to fully categorize. She has been one of Batman's most enduring and complex love interests, appearing across decades of comics and film. Selina operates on her own moral code — one that only she truly understands.
